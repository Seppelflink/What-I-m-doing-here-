# Beta testing guide

Thank you for testing the "Generative AI for Beginners" course. Your goal is to work through it as a real learner and tell us where it was unclear, too slow, or broken. You are testing the course, not being tested.

## Who we need

2–3 testers who are new to generative AI. No coding experience is required for Modules 01–04 and 07–10. Modules 05–06 need Node.js 18 or later.

## What to do

1. Pick the modules you will test. Ideally, complete Modules 01–03 plus one or two others. Each module takes 30–90 minutes (see the [course overview](../README.md)).
2. For each module, read `slides.md`, then do `exercise.md`. Try your own answer before looking at any sample solution.
3. Use a free chat tool or the mock options the exercises describe. You do not need a paid API key. Never paste secrets, real personal data, or confidential material into any AI tool.
4. Note the time you spend and every point where you got stuck, guessed, or had to look something up.
5. Fill in one copy of the [feedback form](feedback-form.md) per module and send it back.

## How to send feedback

- Preferred: open a GitHub issue using the form's content, or paste it into a comment.
- Alternative: send the completed form to the course owner privately.

Remove anything private before sharing.

## What happens next

Feedback is turned into fixes in small pull requests. Reports are summarized in `plan.md` without personal details. Tell us if you prefer not to be named.
