Sample solution hints

These snippets are teaching examples only. A keyword or regex check catches only patterns it knows; it cannot establish that an output is safe or fair. Use it as one signal alongside policy, testing, and human review where appropriate.

- Example filter (regex pseudo-code):
  if (output.matches(/\b(violent|hate|slur)\b/i)) {
    flagForReview(output)
  }

- Example mitigation: change prompt to add a safety instruction:
  "System: refuse to produce hateful or violent content. Answer politely and safely."

- Keep instructor solutions out of learners' default view; use assets/solutions/ or a hidden folder for full references.
